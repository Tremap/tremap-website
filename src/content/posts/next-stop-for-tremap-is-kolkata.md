Great day in the care of UKRI,  Innovate UK and the British High Commission, presenting to a wonderful mix of representatives of the private and public sector in West Bengal.

Tomorrow we’re off to New Town, to see how this satellite city of Kolkata has developed, one of India’s showcase Smart Green Cities!

![](/blog/next-stop-for-tremap-is-kolkata/e719a6-e2a7de33144f49b7916574350738e362.jpg)

What’s striking about the Indian delegates we’ve met is their enthusiasm and positivity.  No mystery why India might be one of the world’s fastest growing economies!  I had questions in my mind, too, coming to India: is green management and urban forestry a “thing” here, yet?  Very much so.  And this sector is set to grow quickly.  Tremap is delighted to be part of that growth curve!
