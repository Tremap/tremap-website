Tremap kicked-off in Chennai, India, today, on Day One of its seven day delegation tour to present our solutions in India.  Richard Maxwell presented the Tremap TEM (Tree Establishment Monitoring) platform and GreenSpaces urban forestry management software to a wide section of public and private sector industry leaders from the state of Tamil Nadu.

![](/blog/b2b-conference-in-chennai/e719a6-c28ca124538047bb9e632ca7e9597630.jpg)

![](/blog/b2b-conference-in-chennai/e719a6-1b7cff2cc8a14f3a82b762eb18e06ebb.jpg)

A wonderfully warm and positive reception from the Indian participants!  The nascent Indian urban green infrastructure management scene is going to explode in the next years, and there are some wonderful opportunities for collaboration, here.

Big shout-out to UKRI Innovate UK for some stellar work behind the scenes, organising the event! They have certainly brought together the right mix of people.
