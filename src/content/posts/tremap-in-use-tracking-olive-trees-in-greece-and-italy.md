We're delighted to welcome BioCoS to the family of Tremap users! BioCoS, based on the island of Crete, Greece, provides varietal identification and DNA traceability for olive trees throughout the Mediterranean countries. They're using Tremap's great new embedded web map feature to build client portfolio maps and provide added-value marketing tools to their clients. This is just the kind of use case we had in mind when we built Tremap, and we love to see it in action!

Check out BioCoS [here](https://www.biocos.gr/).
