Last month Tremap attended Theo Crutchley-Mack's art exhibition at Heligan Gardens!

![](/blog/tremap-x-theo-cruthley-mack/7ca40b-e3ac0733701645adaf0a8a1644a0908c.jpg)

_Theo Crutchley-Mack at Heligan with his new book 'A Story of Cornish Trees"._

Tremap is delighted to be featured in Theo's book 'A Story of Cornish Trees' with eight iconic trees in Cornwall. You can find the location of these trees using the Tremap app.

Tremap's CreativeConnect feature allows artists to attach their creative work to trees and advertise to a global audience through Tremap.

![](/blog/tremap-x-theo-cruthley-mack/943aed-fc0bed9fe12a4de4bae4d79e4e65517c.jpg)

_The Tremap Webmap._

CreativeConnect will soon allow artists to link and sell their work through our global platform!
